Our Story

Born in a Home,
Built on Care

"We didn't start a factory. We started a practice — the quiet, careful art of making something honest."

DEV Herbal Soap began the way most beautiful things do — at home, with intention. In the heart of Jaipur, Rajasthan, we began crafting herbal soaps by hand, inspired by India's long tradition of natural wellness — and have since grown into a small home-care brand, making dhoop, liquid detergent, dish wash and hand wash alongside our soaps.

Every product is made in small batches, with natural ingredients chosen for their purity and purpose — neem for cleansing, rose for softness, saffron for radiance, jasmine for calm. No shortcuts. No harsh chemicals. Just the good of nature, shaped by hand.

We are a young brand, six months old and growing — not in size, but in care. And we believe that good things are worth making slowly.

The Art of
Making by Hand

There are no machines involved. Every bar of DEV soap is shaped by hand, in a home in Jaipur. The process is slow by intention — because care cannot be rushed.

We select each botanical with purpose. Neem, valued in Indian tradition for generations. Rose for softness. Saffron for radiance. Multani mitti for deep cleansing. Ingredients that Indian homes have trusted for generations.

Inspired by India

Tradition
in Modern Form

India has always known how to care for the skin. Long before serums and synthetic fragrances, there was neem, chandan, gulab, haldi, and kesar — ingredients grown in soil, harvested with knowledge, and used with intention.

DEV Herbal Soap carries that wisdom forward. Our formulas are inspired by the traditional herbal practices of Rajasthan — presented in a clean, modern form that fits naturally into daily life.
